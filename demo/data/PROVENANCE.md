# Lab content provenance

The 26 lab records and their checksum fixture come from the neighboring BugR working checkout. That checkout recorded the official URLs and curricula on 4 November 2026. Each record carries its public source URL and source HTML SHA-256.

The demo preserves the approved display names, categories, paths, curriculum headings, tasks, tools, capstones and proof outputs. `verify.mjs` compares rendered phase and proof checksums against `official-labs.json`. The four homepage artifact studies illustrate the documented types of work. The medical chart is synthetic.

The BRD sets the shared offer at ₹9,999 per lab, nine weeks, cohort 30 and STARTING SOON. The feature branch's lead source allowlist uses Cloud and DevOps, IoT and Robotics, and Quality and Safety. The demo uses those approved names consistently.
